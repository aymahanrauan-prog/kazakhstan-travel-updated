Kazakh culture and traditions

Explore nomadic heritage, traditional music and the culture of hospitality.

Cultural highlights

The Nomad Yurt

A portable traditional dwelling designed for a mobile nomadic lifestyle.

A yurt has a wooden lattice frame covered with felt. The round opening at the top, the shanyrak, lets in light and air, and the whole home can be packed and moved with the herds.

National Dombra

A two-stringed instrument closely associated with Kazakh musical heritage.

The dombra is played with a strumming technique and accompanies kuis, instrumental pieces that tell stories. Kazakhstan celebrates National Dombra Day on the first Sunday of July.

Kazakh Hospitality

A cultural tradition of welcoming guests with food, tea and respect.

Guests are traditionally welcomed with tea, baursaks and a shared meal at the dastarkhan, the festive table. Refusing the invitation to sit and eat is considered impolite.

Top 10 Attractions in Kazakhstan

Charyn Canyon (Almaty Region)

A unique natural monument featuring dramatic sedimentary rock formations and the famous Valley of Castles.

Kolsai and Kaindy Lakes

Mountain lakes with turquoise waters, Tian Shan spruce forests and the famous submerged forest of Kaindy.

Baiterek Tower and Astana

An iconic symbol of the capital with an observation deck inside a golden sphere.

Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi

A major historical and spiritual monument in Turkestan and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Bozzhyra Tract (Mangystau)

A striking landscape of white chalk cliffs and rock formations on the Ustyurt Plateau.

Medeu & Shymbulak

A mountain recreation area near Almaty with the Medeu skating rink and Shymbulak ski resort.

Altyn-Emel National Park

Known for the Singing Dune and unusual landscapes of the Katutau and Aktau mountains.

Burabay National Park

A scenic region of pine forests, lakes and granite mountains in northern Kazakhstan.

Katon-Karagay & Mount Belukha

A large protected area in the Altai region with taiga, alpine meadows and mountain landscapes.

Baikonur Cosmodrome

A historic space-launch facility associated with major milestones in the history of space exploration.

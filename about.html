About Kazakhstan Travel

We help travelers discover the nature, history and hospitality of Kazakhstan.

Our mission

Kazakhstan is a country of wide steppes, snow-capped mountains and warm people. Our goal is to make it easy to plan a trip: browse destinations, compare tours, check the weather and get in touch with a local guide.

Whether you want an active mountain trip or a quiet evening with the dombra, we help you find it.

What we care about

Local knowledge

Every tour is built together with guides who know the region.

Clear information

Prices, durations and what is included are always shown up front.

Respect for culture

We introduce visitors to traditions in a respectful, authentic way.
